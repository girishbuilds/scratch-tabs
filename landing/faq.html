Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about Scratch Tabs. Can't find your answer? Reach out on Discord.

Is Scratch Tabs really free?

Yes, Scratch Tabs is completely free and will remain free forever. No premium features, no paywalls, no hidden costs. We believe powerful developer tools should be accessible to everyone.

If the app has helped your productivity, consider supporting it with a coffee.

Is Scratch Tabs open-source?

Yes. The project is open-source and available on GitHub.

Does it really work offline? How?

Yes. All processing happens directly in your browser using JavaScript - no data is ever sent to a server. Your JSON, passwords, and everything else stays on your machine.

Supported browsers can install Scratch Tabs as an app from their browser menu. The Progressive Web App support adds explicit app metadata and service-worker caching, so after the first successful load the app shell can launch from your desktop or home screen without relying on normal browser cache behaviour.

Do I need to create an account?

No account required. Scratch Tabs stores everything locally in your browser's IndexedDB. Just launch the app and start working immediately.

What browsers are supported?

Scratch Tabs works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend using the latest version for the best experience.

Does Spatial Canvas work on mobile?

Canvas editing currently requires a desktop-sized window or pane. Canvas documents and images remain stored locally, while YouTube and Vimeo content loads only when you choose Play.

How do I share or move my work?

Text tabs can be shared through privacy-first URLs where compressed data stays in the hash fragment (#). Canvas documents and their local images can be moved between browsers through workspace export and import.

What makes it different from other tools?

Unlike other tools that require accounts or send data to servers, Scratch Tabs is 100% client-side. It combines data editing, conversion, and specialized engineering tablets in one unified, privacy-focused environment.

I'm not a developer, is it for me?

Absolutely! Many non-developers use Scratch Tabs for its Pomodoro timer, secure password generation, clipboard management, and focus-oriented text editing.

How can I provide feedback?

We welcome feedback via GitHub Issues or our Discord community.
